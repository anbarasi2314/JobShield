About

What is JobShield?

JobShield is an AI-assisted job safety analysis platform designed to help students and job seekers identify potentially suspicious job and internship offers.

PROBLEM

Job seekers increasingly encounter misleading offers

Students, fresh graduates, and first-time applicants are frequent targets for fraudulent job and internship postings — often because they're less familiar with what a normal hiring process looks like, and under pressure to land something quickly.

SOLUTION

Analyze offers before you act on them

JobShield takes a job description, a posting URL, or a screenshot, checks it against common scam indicators — payment requests, unrealistic salary, urgency tactics, missing company details, and more — and returns an explained, indicator-based risk score instead of a blunt "scam / not scam" verdict.

TECHNOLOGY

Simple, transparent, and built to grow

This build focuses on a complete, functional frontend. The current risk engine runs entirely in your browser using a transparent, weighted keyword/heuristic model — visible in js/engine.js — so the whole flow works standalone for demos, without an API key or backend. It's designed to be swapped for a real AI-assisted backend without changing any screen downstream.

DISCLAIMER

An assessment, not a verdict

JobShield provides an automated, indicator-based risk assessment. It is not a definitive determination that any offer is or is not fraudulent. Always verify employers independently — through official websites, direct contact, and trusted job platforms — before sharing personal information or money.
