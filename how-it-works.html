How JobShield works

From a suspicious offer to a plain-language explanation of the risks, in a few simple steps.

01

Submit

Paste text, enter a URL, or upload an image of the job offer.

02

Analyze

JobShield checks the content against common scam indicators and signals.

03

Detect

It weighs payment, salary, contact, urgency, sensitive document requests, and missing company details.

04

Understand

You get a risk score, breakdown, and explanation of what triggered it.
