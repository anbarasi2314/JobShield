Job Scam Safety Guide

The patterns to watch for, and a quick checklist to run through before you apply anywhere new.

Common Job Scam Signs

Any one of these alone isn't proof — but the more that show up together, the more caution is warranted.

Asking for moneyRegistration fees, training kits, or "refundable" deposits before you're even hired.
Unrealistic salaryPay that's well above market rate for the stated role, hours, or experience level.
Fake or skipped interviewsYou're "hired" instantly, or interviewed only via chat with no verification.
Suspicious email addressesRecruiting from a free personal email instead of an official company domain.
No company websiteYou can't find the employer anywhere outside the posting itself.
Pressure to act immediatelyCountdown language, "limited seats," or urgency designed to stop you from checking further.
Requests for banking informationAccount numbers, OTPs, or card details asked for early in the process.
